## Higgsfield (Canvas Node System, Cinema Studio, Elements/Soul ID, Ref Strength, AI Co-Director, Camera/Optics/Physics/Motion, MCSLA, Genre/Pacing/Mood, Start/End Frame, Music Sync, Variants)
- **Canvas Node System**: "Infinite board / node editor: Any prompt, image, video, reference, or prior generation becomes a draggable, connectable node. Chain: prompt feeds image, image feeds video, style branches into variations — so a whole campaign or scene becomes one continuous pipeline rather than a folder of disconnected outputs." "Multiple people can drop nodes, chain pipelines, and generate at the same time. Every version is saved automatically, and comments stay attached to the specific nodes." "Any canvas can be saved as a reusable template — for ad variants, character sheets, storyboards." "Soul ID characters, uploaded products, brand references, and any of your previous generations all drop in as nodes. That means consistent characters across an entire campaign and on-brand visuals without re-uploading the same reference into every shot." "All models run inside the graph... Generate a character in Soul, animate it in Kling, upscale through another node — all in one canvas." "Building the workflow itself is free. Credits only charged when a node generates."
- **Cinema Studio Directing**: "Standalone professional environment that simulates a physical film studio, giving you control over optics, physics, and motion." "AI co-director (Higgs / Mr. Higgs) understands your project — characters, locations, style, and camera settings. Describe a scene in plain language and he'll break it into shots, adjust settings, and populate your prompt — all in real time." "50+ specific cinematic camera presets (static, handheld, push, pan, dolly, orbit, drone, crane, eyes-in/mouth-in extreme close-ups, etc.)." "Lens type, focal length, aperture, camera body simulation for optics (bokeh, depth, distortion, mix 16mm grit with Anamorphic sharpness)." "Genre selection influences pacing, motion energy, and camera behavior... shapes pacing, motion energy, and overall visual tone." "Hero Frame First philosophy: perfecting your still image before you animate... lock in the lighting, mood, and lens character, then extend that exact frame into motion." "Start & End Frame for motion/continuity lock." "Native joint audio-video with lip-sync in integrations (Seedance)." "MCSLA formula (widely referenced in prompt skill)."
- **Elements / Ref Strength / Variants / Music**: "Elements (characters, locations, props) as reusable @tagged nodes/sheets — multi-angle, outfit/palette/motion." "Soul ID / Soul Cast for consistent AI actors with emotions, skin textures." "Music Video genre + joint audio-video." "Variants via canvas templates/patterns + named canvas."
- **Workflow / Secret Sauce**: "Treat the process like a real shoot, where you first establish a strong Hero Frame... then move into animation with a clear plan for optics, motion, and plot." "Directed, not generated" via explicit controls + reusable Elements + node continuity + AI director.
